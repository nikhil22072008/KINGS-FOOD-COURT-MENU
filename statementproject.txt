
**# Project Statement**

**## Problem Statement**

Sometimes it can be difficult to keep track of the food we want to order and the total price.

This project is made to help users see the food menu, choose food items, add them to the order and check the total amount.

**## Scope**

This project can be used for a small food court or restaurant to take simple food orders.

**## Target Users**

- Students

- Customers

- Food court workers

**## High-Level Features**

- Show food menu

- Select food

- Add food to order

- See the order

- Calculate total price

- Checkout

- Confirm order

- Cancel order
